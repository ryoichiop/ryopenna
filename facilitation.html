Transforming conversations into results

Facilitation of strategic conversations

The meetings that decide things: strategic planning, alignment between areas, decisive board sessions. Structure, rhythm, and the right questions, so that voices that arrive separate leave as one direction, with decisions that survive the walk out of the room.

Leadership development

Tailor-made immersions and programs for leadership teams: listening, feedback, and the difficult conversations every team keeps postponing. The growth shows up where it matters, in how people start talking to each other.

Executive coaching

Confidential, individual work with executives on the conversations they lead: the ones that stalled, the ones that keep repeating, the ones that define careers. Listening that goes back and forth until the change shows up in the calendar, not just in the intention.

Conversant

This work happens at Conversant, as a global consultant and facilitator: a consultancy that has spent three decades studying how conversations drive organizational performance, alongside leadership teams at some of the world's largest organizations.
